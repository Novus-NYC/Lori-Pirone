# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are parents and caregivers looking for an imaginative children's book about growth, patience, and transformation. Educators, librarians, event organizers, and readers interested in Lori Pirone's broader creative work are secondary audiences.

## Product Purpose

The website is Lori Pirone's official author presence. Its first job is to introduce _Cute Little Caterpillar_, help adults understand why the story is meaningful for young readers, and make ordering the book easy. It also establishes Lori as a writer, spiritual guide, poet, and creator whose work connects nature, imagination, and inner growth.

## Positioning

Lori's work links a playful caterpillar-to-butterfly story for children with a broader creative practice centered on nature, transformation, wonder, and spiritual reflection.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive while choosing a book for home, bedtime, a classroom, a library, or an event. They should be able to understand the book quickly, verify key details, order it through the existing Amazon destination, learn about Lori, and contact her for questions, events, or bulk orders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Use the existing Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, and Vercel-oriented project structure.
- Build a polished multi-page website from the Novus starter rather than copying or incrementally reskinning the existing WordPress site.
- Preserve important verified information from the existing website.
- Use the README checklist and the repository's `AGENTS.md` delivery standard.
- Provide a sticky or fixed navigation, responsive mobile navigation, route-specific metadata, a favicon, restrained motion, and a complete footer with the exact linked text `Made by Novus`.
- Keep “Order the book” as the primary conversion action.
- Do not publish placeholder statistics, fabricated testimonials, invented social profiles, or unverifiable claims.
- The final route architecture will be confirmed with the chosen design direction. Likely core routes are Home, The Book, About Lori, and Contact.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name Lori Pirone.
- Preserve the _Cute Little Caterpillar_ identity, Lori's portrait, the current logo, relevant book artwork, and caterpillar transformation imagery when source quality supports production use.
- Preserve the themes of nature, transformation, imagination, wonder, patience, and dreams.
- Keep the voice warm, imaginative, reassuring, and clear for adults choosing for children.
- The existing site's visual treatment is reference material, not a visual system that must be retained.

## Product Principles

1. Make the book and its transformation story understandable in the first viewport.
2. Give parents and caregivers a clear, repeated path to order without turning the site into a hard sell.
3. Let Lori's authentic imagery and creative worldview carry the experience.
4. Preserve real information while removing placeholders, duplication, and unsupported claims.
5. Make every route purposeful, accessible, responsive, and visually connected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, useful image alternatives, generous touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
